SCARline

The plug-and-play platform for building, running and reviewing driving simulator studies of in-vehicle HMIs.

SCARline sits on top of a driving simulator and takes care of the technical plumbing — simulator control, sensors, displays, logging and data export — so researchers and designers can focus on the research question and the interaction they want to test.

The SCARline Hub

One workspace for every study

Set up participants, conditions, simulator, sensors and displays in one place, then run and review every session from the same screen. A built-in checklist shows exactly what's missing before a study can go live.
